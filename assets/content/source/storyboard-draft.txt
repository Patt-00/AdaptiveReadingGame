-Princess Hailey: The host, planning to confront all the people who wronged her at the party. She gets poisoned in the middle of a speech and will soon die if help doesn't arrive soon.
Motive for the party:  She is a princess who genuinely cares for her kingdom but was put on a pedestal (worshipped and harassed) for so long that she was tired of it all and was planning to abdicate her throne but gets poisoned before announcing.


  



-Lady Amelia: Hailey's Lady-in-waiting. She is Hailey's right hand woman who is reserved and shy but empathetically perceptive.
Possible motive: Hailey ‘bought’ Amelia’s loyalty by clearing her family's debt. She could've killed the princess to be free or tired of feeling like a “charity case”.
  





-Lady Eris: Hailey's fellow noblelady and best friend. She is Hailey's confidante and the source of information in gossip.
Possible motive: Eris is very envious of Hailey's beauty and influence, and often spreads negative rumors about the princess. As the rumors get traced back, Eris’ reputation and name could be ruined forever or could end with treason.


  



-Sir Davor: Hailey's childhood friend and knight. He has always been protecting his mistress' since he was a squire and is a renowned hero.
Possible motive: He is obsessed with Hailey to a toxic degree and is upset that she is betrothed to another, driving him to manic desperation. His obsession twists into a "if I can't have you, no one will" mindset after she rejects his advances. 


  



-Prince Owen: Hailey's fiance. He is from another kingdom and is soon to marry her. He is cunning,handsome, and just.
Possible motive: His marriage with Hailey is arranged, and hates the idea that he has to get married just to be king of his kingdom. The marriage contract secretly strips Owen's kingdom of its sovereign resources or rights, making him a puppet king. If she dies after entering his kingdom but before their marriage, the contract could be deemed fulfilled.


  



-Lord Robert: Owen’s secretary and cousin. He serves as Owen’s aide in his princely duties and acts like an ambassador or a spokesperson in his absence.
Possible motive: Robert believes Owen is weak and that marrying into Hailey’s kingdom will destabilize their kingdom. He tries to position himself, frame Owen, and take his place to secure the throne. 


  



-The Mage Emrys: Owen bodyguard. They are a mage who lives reclusively and has an intimidating reputation, having the knowledge of forbidden grimoires.
Possible motive: Hailey discovered Emrys’s forbidden knowledge and intended to use her speech to expose them, ensuring their public execution to gain political favor with her anti-magic nobles. Emrys acted purely out of self-defense to destroy the evidence/silence her before she could name them.
